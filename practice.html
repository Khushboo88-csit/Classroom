Amazon.com, Inc. is an American multinational technology company founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994, focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence.
       It is one of the "Big Five" American tech companies and dominates online retail, with a massive share of U.S. e-commerce.  

Key aspects of Amazon include:
"The Everything Store": Started as an online bookstore, it now sells a massive, diverse range of products, with 62% of units sold in Q4 2024 originating from third-party sellers.
Amazon Web Services (AWS): The company's most profitable division, providing cloud computing services to businesses, governments, and individuals.
Subscribers & Retail: Operates the Amazon Prime subscription service, which offers fast, free shipping, alongside Prime Video and Music.
Hardware: Manufactures electronics such as the Kindle, Echo (Alexa), Fire TV, and tablets.
Logistics & Physical Stores: Uses a vast network of fulfillment centers for shipping and owns physical locations like Whole Foods Market.
Subsidiaries: Owns major companies including Twitch, IMDb, Ring, and MGM Holdings. 

Amazon has a "Day 1" mentality, focusing heavily on customer experience, innovation, and long-term thinking, rather than short-term profit.
